Hi, I’m Tim Pinoy. I’m a technology professional with a background in regulatory reporting software consulting and presales.

Like many others, I first fell in love with computers through video games. I was lucky enough to grow up learning how to launch games like Ken’s Labyrinth, Skunny: Save Our Pizzas, and Lemmings from floppy disks in DOS. But the real trigger was the AoE2 scenario editor, where I spent countless hours making custom maps. From there I learned html, css, and some javascript to make websites for friends and family.

This site is a place to keep track of what I learn and the occasional notes from a book, podcast, or whatever else happens to catch my interest. It's meant as an incentive to keep my notes more polished so that I'm able to share them with others.

Some of my other interests are reading science fiction & fantasy, and listening to metal.
